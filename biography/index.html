Ethem Alpaydın received his BSc from the Department of Computer Engineering of Bogazici University in 1987 and the degree of Docteur es Sciences from Ecole Polytechnique Federale de Lausanne in 1990. He did his postdoctoral work at the International Computer Science Institute, Berkeley between Feb-Aug 1991.

Between 1991 and 2019, he was a Professor at the Department of Computer Engineering of Bogazici University. Between 2019 and 2026, he was a Professor at the Department of Computer Science at Özyeğin University.

As a visiting researcher, he worked at the Department of Brain and Cognitive Sciences, MIT in 1994, International Computer Science Institute, Berkeley in 1997, IDIAP, Switzerland in 1998, and TU Delft in 2014.

He was Fulbright Senior Scholar in 1997/1998 and received the Research Excellence Award from the Bogazici University Foundation in 1998 (junior faculty) and 2008 (senior faculty), the Young Scientist Award from the Turkish Academy of Sciences in 2001, and the Scientific Encouragement Award from the Turkish Scientific and Technical Research Council in 2002.  He is a Member of The Science Academy, Turkey, since 2012, and a Member of Academia Europaea since 2023.

His books are

  • Introduction to Machine Learning was published by The MIT Press. Its four editions appeared respectively in 2004, 2010, 2014, and 2020. The book was translated into Chinese, German, and Turkish.
  • Machine Learning: The New AI was published by The MIT Press as part of the Essential Knowledge Series in September 2016; Japanese, Russian, Korean, Chinese, Turkish, and Croation editions have subsequently been published. Machine Learning: Revised and Updated Edition was published in August 2021; Finnish edition has since been published.
  • Fundamentals of Probability and Statistics for Machine Learning was published by The MIT Press in December 2025.
